Our story

One bean. Done right.

Since 2018

From a garage drum to one perfect roast.

Julian Vance started roasting on a restored 1968 drum roaster with one goal: a coffee that's smooth enough to drink black and rich enough to taste like dessert.

After years of cupping, he stopped looking. Midnight Velvet is the result, and it's the only coffee we sell.

Julian Vance

How it's made

Three steps. No shortcuts.

  1. 1

    Sourced direct

    Hand-picked at 1,850m. We pay our farm partners 40% above market.

  2. 2

    Roasted in small batches

    15 kg at a time, to a medium-dark that brings out the chocolate.

  3. 3

    Nitrogen-flushed and on its way to you within 24 hours.
